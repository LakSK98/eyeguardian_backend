General Eye-Care Guidance

Daily Eye Care

  • Use comfortable and appropriate lighting for reading and screen work.
  • Take regular visual breaks during prolonged near work.
  • Avoid rubbing the eyes unnecessarily.
  • Use prescribed spectacles or contact lenses as advised.

Screen & Reading Habits

  • Maintain a comfortable viewing distance from digital screens.
  • Use the 20-20-20 rule during prolonged screen use.
  • Avoid excessively bright or dim display settings.
  • Seek an eye examination if persistent visual difficulty is noticed.

Professional Eye-Care Recommendation

This screening does not replace a comprehensive eye examination. If symptoms are persistent, worsening, severe, or associated with significant visual changes, professional ophthalmic assessment is recommended.
